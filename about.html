How Feane Was Built

Founded in 2009 by Chef Marco Feane, we started a small burger stand with one goal: serve food so good people

2009

The first Burger stand

Chef Macro Feane opens a small weekend burger stand at the local with just 3 menu item and a charcoal. Line form within weekss.

2012

First Brick-and-Mortar Location

After 3 years of savings, Feane opens its first physical restaurant seating 40 guests. The signature "Feane Stack" burger becomes a local sensation.

2016

Online ordering launched

Feane launches its own wesite and delivery app. making it possible to order the the famous burgers from anywhere in the city ithin 30 minutes.

2019

Secound & Third location Open

Riding the wave of growing demands, two new branches open on the north and side of the city. Staff grows from to 65 emplyees.

2026

50,000th Customer Celevrated

Feane reaches a major milestone - 50,000 unique customer served. A celebration event with freee burger and music marks the occasion.
